Mile End Kicks

🎬 Netflix · Streaming July 13, 2026
"If you liked Roommates, director Chandler Levack's second feature of the year is hitting the streamer and it's an absolute vibe. Barbie Ferreira gives a career-best performance as a music journalist falling hard for Montreal's indie scene — and maybe the guitarist too. This is the kind of film Blockbuster would've had on the Staff Picks shelf right next to the register." — Alex, Blockbuster Staff (Employee of the Month, 1998)
Director Chandler Levack follows up Roommates with this warm, scrappy, music-soaked indie gem. Set in Montreal's Mile End neighborhood, it's a love letter to local bands, late-night diners, and the beautiful chaos of figuring out what you actually want. Shot on film, scored by actual Montreal artists, and featuring a breakout turn from Barbie Ferreira.
