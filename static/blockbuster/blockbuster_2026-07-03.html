Project Hail
Mary
Prime Video 🎬 Streaming May 13 🎥 Mar 20 94% 🍅
Okay, I know this one's been on the wall for a bit, but trust me — half the store hasn't discovered it yet and they're asking for it every Friday night. Ryan Gosling plays a lone astronaut who wakes up with amnesia on a ship light-years from Earth, and what unfolds is the most emotionally satisfying sci-fi epic since Interstellar. Phil Lord and Christopher Miller direct with their signature blend of heart and humor, and Gosling delivers a career-best performance. It's 156 minutes and you won't check your phone once. Return it by Sunday — but you'll probably want to watch it twice.
