👔 STAFF PICK

The Dink

Look, I know what you're thinking — a pickleball movie? But trust me on this one. The Dink is the most fun you'll have on your couch this weekend. Josh Greenbaum (who gave us Strays and Barb and Star) directs with perfect comedic timing, and the cast is absolutely stacked: Jake Johnson brings his signature slacker charm, Mary Steenburgen steals every scene, Ed Harris plays against type as a grizzled pickleball nemesis, and Ben Stiller produces AND appears in a gloriously self-deprecating supporting role. Plus, actual tennis legends John McEnroe and Andy Roddick have cameos. It's Dodgeball meets Kingpin with paddles — absurd, heartfelt, and way better than it has any right to be.

"I haven't laughed this hard at a sports comedy since Happy Gilmore. The Dink is an ace."
— Jamie, Blockbuster Video (25 years behind the counter)
