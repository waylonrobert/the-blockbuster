Hamnet

Chloé Zhao  ·  Historical Drama  ·  PG-13

🔥 STEPHEN KING
Max 🎬 July 10

The Long Walk

Francis Lawrence  ·  Dystopian Thriller  ·  R

Stephen King's brutal 1979 novel finally gets the screen treatment it deserves. In a totalitarian America, 100 teenage boys walk until only one remains. Directed by Francis Lawrence (The Hunger Games), this is a white-knuckle survival thriller that never lets up.

Starring: Cooper Hoffman, David Jonsson, Charlie Plummer

⭐ OSCAR WINNER
Peacock 🎬 July 9

Love Hurts

Jonathan Eusebio  ·  Action Comedy  ·  R

Oscar-winner Ke Huy Quan proves he can do anything in this wildly entertaining action-comedy. A mild-mannered realtor's past as a hitman comes roaring back when his former partner-in-crime resurfaces. Think John Wick meets Everything Everywhere All at Once — pure joy.

Starring: Ke Huy Quan, Ariana DeBose, Daniel Wu

🚀 SPACE THRILLER

I.S.S.

Gabriela Cowperthwaite  ·  Sci-Fi Thriller  ·  R

Tensions ignite aboard the International Space Station when nuclear war breaks out on Earth below. American and Russian cosmonauts receive orders to take control — by any means necessary. Claustrophobic, tense, and utterly gripping.

Starring: Ariana DeBose, Chris Messina, Pilou Asbæk

💔 COLLEEN HOOVER
Peacock 🎬 July 10

Vanessa Caswill  ·  Romance / Drama  ·  PG-13

Colleen Hoover's heart-wrenching bestseller comes to the screen. After serving five years for a tragic mistake, Kenna Rowan returns to the town where it all went wrong, hoping to reunite with her daughter — and finding unexpected love along the way.

👔 STAFF PICK
Netflix 🎬 July 6

Hamnet

Chloé Zhao · Historical Drama · PG-13

From two-time Oscar winner Chloé Zhao (Nomadland, Eternals) comes an exquisite adaptation of Maggie O'Farrell's prize-winning novel. Jessie Buckley and Paul Mescal deliver career-best performances as Agnes and William Shakespeare, parents grappling with the unbearable loss of their 11-year-old son Hamnet — the boy whose name would echo through literary eternity. Luminous, deeply felt, and visually ravishing, this is the kind of film that reminds you why cinema matters.

"A luminous, deeply felt portrait of love, art, and loss — Zhao's finest work since Nomadland."

— The Blockbuster Staff

Starring: Jessie Buckley, Paul Mescal, Joe Alwyn, Emily Watson
